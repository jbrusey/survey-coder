# Module Pacing and Depth (Positive)
Stream: Positive

## 1. Module Underloaded
**Definition:**
The student indicates that the module's content is too basic, there is too little material covered, or the overall duration of the module (number of weeks or total hours across the term) is too long for the amount of learning provided.

**Include when:**
- Mentions "too basic", "base level", or "nothing new".
- Suggests the module could be completed in less time.
- Indicates they already knew most of the content.
- Specifically refers to the *whole module* as being too long.

**Exclude when:**
- Refers to a single lecture or seminar being too long (e.g., "3 hours is too much for one sitting").
- Refers to the length of breaks.

**Example evidence:**
- "The content was a bit basic."
- "Module should include something new."

**Notes:**
Focus on the *density* and *depth* of the learning material.

---

## 2. Module Overloaded
**Definition:**
The student indicates that there is too much content to cover, the module feels rushed, or the overall teaching time (number of weeks or sessions) is too short to adequately learn the material.

**Include when:**
- Mentions "rushed", "too much content", or "packed".
- Requests additional weeks, extra sessions, or a longer duration for the module.
- Mentions not having enough time to cover everything.

**Exclude when:**
- Refers to the speed of the lecturer's speech (pacing of delivery).
- Refers to technical delays (e.g., "tapping in took too long").

**Example evidence:**
- "A longer duration or extra weeks was needed."
- "It felt very rushed."

**Notes:**
Focus on the *volume* of content relative to the *time* allocated.
