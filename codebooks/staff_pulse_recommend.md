# Staff Pulse: Recommend

Use these codes for responses to “I would recommend CUG as a good place to work”. Apply every code that is clearly supported by the response. Use the exact code names below. A response may receive more than one code.

## I would recommend Coventry University Group as a good place to work.
Explicitly says the respondent would recommend CUG as a good place to work, without a more specific reason being the main point.

## Used to recommend
Says the respondent previously recommended CUG but no longer does, or has stopped recommending it.

## Work is rewarding
Says the work itself is meaningful, worthwhile, or rewarding.

## Wld recommend my team
Would recommend the respondent's team or local colleagues specifically.

## Facilities and people
Identifies facilities and/or the people at CUG as positive reasons to recommend it.

## Lack of facilities e.g. Ryton
Criticises inadequate, unavailable, or poor facilities, including the Ryton site.

## I can work from home
Identifies the ability or opportunity to work from home as a positive reason.

## Negative - Poor reputation
Says CUG's poor reputation or public image makes it difficult to recommend.

## Neative - Managers do not respect/care about staff
Says managers do not respect, support, or care about staff.

## Negative - Uncertainty and lack of job security
Describes uncertainty, insecurity, or concern about continued employment.

## Negative - Would actively discourage
Says the respondent would actively discourage someone from working at CUG.

## Negative - Way change, including org change handled
Criticises how change, including organisational change, is planned, communicated, or handled.

## Negative - Decisions not made fairly
Says decisions are unfair, inconsistent, biased, or lack due process.

## Neutral - Depends e.g. where you work
Says whether they would recommend CUG depends on circumstances, such as team, department, or location.

## Negative - Lack of operational resilience
Says CUG is unable to maintain or restore effective operations when problems arise.

## Negative - Pay/Lack of reward/recongition
Criticises pay, rewards, or recognition for staff contributions.

## Negative - Lack of progression
Criticises limited career progression or advancement opportunities.

## Negative - Lack of development opportunities
Criticises limited access to training, learning, or professional development.

## Negative - Do not feel appreciated/ valued
Says staff or the respondent do not feel appreciated or valued.

## Negative - Lack of staff
Describes understaffing, vacancies, or insufficient staffing levels.

## Negative - Lack of transparency
Criticises a lack of openness, clear communication, or transparency.

## Negative - Management style e.g. micro-managament, autocracy
Criticises management style, including micromanagement, excessive control, or autocratic behaviour.

## Negative - Poor work ethic/ not ambitious enough
Criticises poor work ethic, low standards of effort, or insufficient ambition.

## Negative - Workload too high
Says the overall workload is excessive.

## Negative - Workload not evenly distributed
Says work is distributed unfairly or unevenly among staff.

## Negative - negative experience
Describes a generally negative experience at CUG that is not captured by a more specific code.

## Negative - Leadership failings
Criticises leadership failures or ineffective leadership beyond a specific issue covered by another code.

## Negative - Low morale/wellbeing
Describes low staff morale, poor wellbeing, or harm to staff wellbeing.

## Negative - Hard to get things done round here
Says processes, systems, or organisational barriers make it difficult to get work done.

## Negative - Internal-only hires restricts candidate quality
Says internal-only hiring or restricted recruitment reduces the quality or range of candidates.

## Negative - Ts & Cs
Criticises terms and conditions of employment.

## Negative - loss of trust
Says trust in CUG, its leaders, or its decisions has been lost or damaged.

## Negative - People Team
Criticises the People Team or its service, decisions, or conduct.

## Negative - Lack of flexibility
Criticises insufficient flexibility in how, when, or where staff can work.
