# Staff Pulse: Proud

Use these codes for responses to “I feel proud working for CUG”. Apply every code that is clearly supported by the response. Use the exact code names below. A response may receive more than one code.

## I am proud to work for Coventry University Group
Explicitly says the respondent is proud to work for Coventry University Group (CUG), without a more specific reason being the main point.

## Negative - Used to be proud
Says the respondent previously felt proud but no longer does, or that pride has declined.

## Negative press/external perception/reputation
Says negative press, outside perceptions, or CUG's reputation reduce the respondent's pride.

## Enjoy/proud - work I do
Pride or enjoyment is specifically tied to the respondent's own work, role, or contribution.

## Enjoy/proud - team
Pride or enjoyment is specifically tied to the respondent's immediate team or colleagues.

## Enjoy - NITE
Enjoyment or pride is specifically attributed to NITE.

## Proud - students
Pride is specifically tied to CUG's students, their achievements, or their success.

## Proud - achievements/impact
Pride is tied to CUG's achievements, contribution, or positive impact.

## Proud - reputation
Pride is tied to CUG's positive reputation or standing.

## Proud - to work in HE sector
Pride is tied to working in higher education as a sector.

## Staff treated poorly
Says staff are treated badly or unfairly, with this given as a reason pride is reduced.

## Positive reputation
Mentions CUG's good reputation or standing positively, without otherwise expressing pride.

## Negative - Lack of integrity
Reports dishonesty, lack of integrity, or unethical conduct at CUG.

## Negative - Senior leaders
Criticises senior leaders or their actions, decisions, or conduct.

## Negative - Restructure process
Criticises a restructuring or organisational change process.

## Negative - Workload/WAMS
Criticises workload, workload management, or WAMS-related demands.

## Negative - Values/Priorities disconnect
Says CUG's stated values or priorities do not match its actions or actual priorities.

## Negative - Declining conditions
Says working conditions or the employment environment have deteriorated.

## Negative - Lack of pay progression/reward
Criticises pay progression, rewards, or recognition of contribution.

## Negative - Don't feel valued
Says the respondent or staff generally do not feel valued or appreciated.

## Negative - Falling standards
Says standards, quality, or performance at CUG are declining.

## Negative -bureaucracy
Criticises bureaucracy, red tape, or excessive administrative processes.

## Depends
Says pride depends on circumstances, role, team, or another condition without making a clear positive or negative statement.

## Negative - Lack of career progression
Criticises limited opportunities for career advancement.

## Negative - lack of transparency
Criticises a lack of openness, clear communication, or transparency.

## Negative - Departmental politics
Criticises internal politics, factionalism, or conflict between departments.

## Negative - Low morale
Describes low morale or poor staff sentiment.

## Negative - Uncertainty
Describes uncertainty or insecurity about CUG's future, direction, or the respondent's position.

## Negative - People Team
Criticises the People Team or its service, decisions, or conduct.

## Negative - Constant change
Criticises frequent or continuous organisational change.

## Negative - Performance issues not dealt with
Says poor performance or misconduct is not addressed appropriately.

## Negative - Lack of flexibility
Criticises insufficient flexibility in how, when, or where staff can work.

## Negative - Academic Strategy
Criticises CUG's Academic Strategy or its implementation.

## Negative - Facilities/ Room quality
Criticises facilities, buildings, rooms, or the quality or availability of work spaces.
